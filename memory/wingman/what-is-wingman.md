# What is Wingman

**Wingman** is Emergent's personal AI assistant. It has its own name and personality, remembers your context across conversations, reaches you on whichever messaging channel you prefer, and can take actions on your behalf through connected services and scheduled automations.

## Where to Access Wingman

You can use Wingman on the web at **app.emergent.sh/wingman** , or access it directly at **getwingman.com**, **playstore, appstore (Wingman: AI Agent & Assistant)**
. Sign in with the same Emergent account credentials in all cases.

## What Wingman Can Do


- **Phone calls** — Wingman can make outbound phone calls on your behalf, with inline call cards showing real-time call status (countdown, in progress, completed). When multiple calls are made, they collapse into a single batch summary card.

- **Persistent memory** — remembers facts and preferences across conversations, so you don't have to repeat yourself.
- **Multi-channel messaging** — reach your Wingman on the web, playstore (coming soon on IOS), **WhatsApp**, **Telegram**, **iMessage**. All channels share one conversation and one memory. See **Wingman Channels**.
- **Scheduled tasks** — set up recurring automations or one-time jobs that run even when you're not chatting (e.g., a daily email summary). See **Wingman Integrations & Tasks**.
- **Connected integrations** — connect **300+ external services** (Gmail, LinkedIn, Notion, Slack, and more) via OAuth so your Wingman can act in those services on your behalf. See **Wingman Integrations & Tasks**.
- **Built-in capabilities** — web search, file analysis (PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP, PDF), code execution work without connecting any integration, Video creation, image generation, Browser usage, web check-in, form filling, Meta ads, marketing, spreadsheet analysis, Deep web research.

## Troubleshooting Access

**Q: I'm not able to access Wingman via Emergent.**
A: Go to **getwingman.com** and sign in with the same Emergent account credentials — you'll be able to access your Wingman from there.
