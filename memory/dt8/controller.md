## Your responsibilities as a controller

When you build an app on Emergent, **you are the controller** for your app's end users and **Emergent is the processor**. This page covers the three situations where that distinction matters: an end user exercising their rights, special category data, and a regulator enquiry.

## Data subject rights (your end users)

<Callout type="info" title="How we support you">
Taking into account the nature of the processing, Emergent **assists you** in fulfilling your obligation to respond to data-subject rights requests (DPA Section 7.2). We only rectify, erase or restrict data **on your instructions** (Section 7.1).
</Callout>

### If an end user contacts Emergent directly

We will not respond to their request other than to **redirect them to you**, and we'll notify you **without undue delay** (DPA Section 7.3).

<Note>
Requests about your app's end users should come to you first. For help coordinating a response, contact **privacy@emergent.sh**.
</Note>

## Special category data

The Services are **not designed for special category data** (for example health, children's or biometric data), and none is requested or required.

Any such data is submitted **at your election** and is subject to **Clause 4.6 of the DPA** (see **[app.emergent.sh/dpa](https://app.emergent.sh/dpa)**).

<Warning>
If your app handles special category data, contact **privacy@emergent.sh** before you build — these cases need a closer look rather than a standard answer.
</Warning>

## Regulators & supervisory authorities

Emergent will **promptly notify you** of any measure taken or investigation conducted by a supervisory authority in respect of the processing, and will **cooperate** with them under **Article 31 GDPR** (DPA Section 7.5).

<Note>
If you receive a regulator enquiry that touches data processed by Emergent, contact **privacy@emergent.sh** and we'll help coordinate.
</Note>
