## Where your data lives

Your customer data is **processed and stored in the United States and India**.

The processing location for each individual sub-processor is listed at **[app.emergent.sh/subprocessors](https://app.emergent.sh/subprocessors)**.

<Callout type="info" title="Region selection">
Choosing a specific region (for example EU-only hosting) is **not available self-serve** today. If you have a regional requirement, contact **privacy@emergent.sh** and we will come back to you on feasibility and timing.
</Callout>

<Warning>
Please don't assume a region change is instant or free — get confirmation from us in writing before relying on it.
</Warning>

## Who processes your data

The full sub-processor list is published at **[app.emergent.sh/subprocessors](https://app.emergent.sh/subprocessors)**. It names each sub-processor, what we use it for, and where it processes data.

<Callout type="info" title="You are kept informed of changes">
We inform customers of intended additions or replacements by updating the published sub-processor list (DPA Section 8.2). The list is reviewed **at least annually**.
</Callout>

## Emergent stays responsible

We remain **fully liable** to you for each sub-processor's performance, in line with **Article 28(4) GDPR** (DPA Section 8.6). Each sub-processor is bound by data-protection terms at least as protective as those in our DPA.

<Note>
For the current vendor list, purposes and processing locations, always check the live page: **[app.emergent.sh/subprocessors](https://app.emergent.sh/subprocessors)**. If you have a question about a specific vendor, write to **privacy@emergent.sh** and link the entry.
</Note>
